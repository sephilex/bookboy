FAQ

Which formats are supported?

TXT only. Shutong detects encodings such as GBK, Big5 and UTF-8 automatically; if the text looks garbled, choose another encoding from the reader’s menu.

How do I add books?

Import from the Files app or iCloud Drive, share from another app, or add an SMB / WebDAV server in Sources to browse your NAS or computer shares. On iPhone and iPad you can also use Wi-Fi Transfer: open the address shown on screen in a computer’s browser and drag TXT files in.

Wi-Fi Transfer’s address won’t open?

Make sure the computer and the device are on the same network, and allow Shutong in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. Keep the Wi-Fi Transfer screen open while transferring.

iCloud Sync isn’t working?

Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on, and that iCloud Sync is on in Shutong’s Settings. The first sync of a large library can take a few minutes.

Can’t connect to a NAS or shared folder?

Check the address, port, share name or path, user name and password, and make sure the device and server are on the same network or the server accepts connections from it.

Is it free?

Yes. Shutong is completely free, with no in-app purchases, ads or accounts.
