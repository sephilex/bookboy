Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy matches Settings › Privacy in the app.

Data Not Collected

Shutong has no account, ads, analytics or third-party tracking, and collects no data. The developer can’t see your books, reading history or settings.

iCloud Sync

With iCloud Sync on, your book files, bookshelf, reading progress, bookmarks and settings are stored in your own iCloud account (iCloud Drive and a private CloudKit database). Only devices signed in to the same Apple Account can read them, and Apple keeps them under its privacy policy. With iCloud Sync off, this data stays on this device.

Servers and Passwords

SMB and WebDAV servers you add are used only to browse and download the books you choose. Server passwords are stored in your keychain and sync between your devices through iCloud Keychain. Shutong connects only to servers you enter or pick on your local network.

Local Network

Local Network access is used only for Wi-Fi Transfer and to find servers on your local network. Wi-Fi Transfer runs only while its screen is open, and books go straight from a browser on the same network to this device without passing through any outside server.

Feedback Email

When you send feedback by email, your message and the app version, system version and device model added to it are used only to reply to you and improve Shutong.

Crash Reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in Settings, Apple provides the developer with crash reports that don’t identify you. Shutong itself collects no crash information.

Contact

For questions or suggestions, email sephilex@gmail.com. Sending from Settings › Feedback in Shutong adds the app version, system version and device model to help us look into it.
